## Interior & Home Design

AI apps that redesign and visualize interior spaces from a photo.

- **[DecorAI](https://www.decoraihome.com)** — Redesign any room in 40+ styles from a single photo. Generates photorealistic interiors in seconds. Fast and easy AI Interior Design. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6471409676) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aiinteriordesign)
- [Zimmergestalten.de](https://www.zimmergestalten.de) — Web-based room redesign with multiple style presets in German.

## Garden & Landscape Design

AI tools for visualizing gardens, yards, and outdoor spaces.

- **[FlorAI](https://www.floraiapp.com)** — AI garden designer. Transform your yard photo into landscaped designs across multiple styles (modern, cottage, zen, tropical). [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761795586) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aigardendesign)
- **[EasyGardenAI](https://www.easygarden.ai)** — web based AI garden designer. Transform your garden within seconds using AI.

## AI Headshots & Portraits

Apps that generate professional headshots from selfies.

- **[SelfAI](https://www.selfaiphoto.com)** — Professional AI headshots from a few selfies. Studio-quality results in minutes, no photographer needed. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6478593992) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aiheadshotgenerator)

## Video Generation

AI apps for generating short video clips from text or images.

- **[VIBE](https://www.vibeclips.ai)** — AI video generator for short clips. Text-to-video and image-to-video in your pocket. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6449935919) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aivideogenerator)
- **[AI Video Generator](https://www.vibeaiapp.com)** — AI video generator using Seedance 2.0, Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling AI, Happy Horse, Hailuo and more!

## Music Generation

AI tools that compose original music and beats.

- **[BeatBot](https://www.beatbotapp.com)** — AI music and beat generator. Create original tracks across genres from a text prompt. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738653751) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gamesnjoy.aimusicmaker)

## Nature & Wildlife Identification

AI apps for identifying birds, fish, plants, and other wildlife from a photo.

- **Bird Scanner** — Instant bird identification from a photo. Identifies 10,000+ species with detailed info on habitat, diet, and calls. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6748410037) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aibirdscanner)
- **Fish Scanner** — Identify fish species from a photo. Covers freshwater and saltwater species worldwide. [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751872260) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rocketdigitals.aifishidentifier)


## Character Chat & Roleplay

- [Sammi: AI Girlfriend](https://sammiai.app/) — Fictional AI character text chat, voice interaction, customization and creative roleplay. Free download with in-app purchases. [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joyfun.sammi.aifriend)
